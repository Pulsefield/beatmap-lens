# Curator

Propose semantic changes outside routine labeling and independent auditing. Read
the [shared workflow](../agent-workflow.md). `needs-revision` belongs to the labeler;
claim-specific expert questions use the focused queue. Standalone semantic
questions without a claim belong here, not in empty new handoffs or audits.

Read `inbox --server http://127.0.0.1:4176` and
`dispositions --server http://127.0.0.1:4176 --source-sha SOURCE_SHA` through the
exchange CLI. Gather related issues with exact source, original Foundation, and
saved human decisions. The fixed page and persistent outbox handle delivery; the
human does not export files for you. Inspect source before suggesting a definition,
cue, calibration example, or alignment. Agent agreement and community co-occurrence
do not establish semantic equivalence.

Prepare a new proposed Foundation and a concise explanation of:

- The meaning or guidance being changed and its prior version.
- Source-backed positives, near-misses, and unresolved cases with scopes and evidence.
- External correspondences, including direction, scope, and pooling meaning.
- Which older observations need explicit reconsideration if meaning changes.

Keep new semantics proposed until their actual human approval is recorded. The
four-dimension pilot and corrected calibration are already approved; do not ask
for that global approval again merely to record its authorized snapshot. This does
not select whole-difficulty community targets or approve weak alignments. Agent
exchange commands expose no Foundation-approval or human-decision mutation.

Do not rewrite historical Foundations, re-pin observations, or relabel old data.
Full impact tracking and semantic re-review remain later milestones. A delivery
request for a small human sample does not change this semantic approval boundary.
