# Mania pattern judgment

Read what the player follows through **time, action, and organization**: rhythm,
presses/holds/releases, and motifs within larger episodes. The supplied frozen
Foundation defines the labels. Learn their application from source arrangements
and effective human judgments through the harness.

## Judge the supplied episode

Read complete attack groups, intervening rows, entering holds and entry/exit
context. Preserve source times and LN endpoints, including short LNs. Choose
tools when a missing fact or perspective could change the judgment.

Assess each label independently at the assigned scope and rate. Presence,
strength and confidence are separate; several labels can be prominent. Select
evidence while judging, including for `absent`, and explain the decisive relation.
Query misses are not absence. Keep inspected uncertainty `unresolved` and
uninspected dimensions `unreviewed`.

**Trill:** An assigned crop does not create a perceptual episode. Read fixed-group
alternation against surrounding flow: do its speed, extent and entry/exit establish
a recognizable exchange or recurring local figure? Entering or leaving the fixed
groups alone does not articulate a boundary. Brief alternation with blurred
boundaries inside continuing motion can be incidental and absent. Supporting requires weak but definite
exchange, not a structural-match fallback. Brief, clearly articulated exchanges
can still be positive. Neither a separate pause nor dominance is required;
do not invent duration or row-count cutoffs.

In short transitions, require a definite repeated rhythmic figure, not merely
complete coverage by alternating heads. If limited extent and integration with
the continuing phrase leave that figure indistinct, mark absent rather than
supporting. When that distinction is unclear, inspect a time-proportional image
of the review context.

## Find a useful human comparison

Use `find_human_examples` for disputed presence or strength, using source facets
or short comment keywords. ID order is not similarity. Prefer explicit High;
Low is uncertain and missing confidence unrecorded. Open useful cases with
`get_human_example` and inspect their source. When boundaries matter, inspect the
example's review context too. Compare decisive relations in a
positive and counterexample, including differences that could change presence.
Refine retrieval when the comparison misses the question; no fixed pack or quota.

Human decisions apply at their source, scope, rate and Foundation. Preserve original
comments; confirmation does not approve inherited agent reasoning or note selection.
Community tags guide whole-difficulty exploration. Machine judgments remain
hypotheses. Respect example exclusions; report conflicts without changing human
records or pins.
